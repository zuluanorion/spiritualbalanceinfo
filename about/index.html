Dr. Zuluan Orion

Originator of Quantum Transformation · Cognizer of the Light Codes

I was born in Bogotá in 1971. I grew up in a hard home, and the emptiness it left in my heart became the beginning of my spiritual path. I needed to find something, though I didn't know what.

That search took me through more than sixty thousand hours of meditation and more than twenty years living and studying in India and Thailand: yoga, meditation and Ayurvedic medicine in India, Buddhism and healing in Thailand. Along the way an awakening to the Self came, and in nearly thirty years it has not reversed.

Out of that life came the two things I offer. Quantum Transformation is the system I created to guide the soul through its own levels of Consciousness, to the root of what hurts, and back to its light. The Light Codes are a sacred language of syllables and sigils that I received and recognized, and they live at the heart of the Flame Codex.

For two decades I have brought them together in my clinic in Weston, Florida, with Ayurvedic, natural and quantum medicine, and with people from many countries.

I am not the source. Consciousness is. My work is to return you to the light you never lost.
