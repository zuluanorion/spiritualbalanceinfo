Ayurveda

The science of life

AyurvedaThe three doshasPanchakarmaRasayana

Ayurveda does not look only at a symptom. It looks at you.

Ayurveda is the traditional medicine of India, and it is thousands of years old. Its name joins two words: ayus, life, and veda, knowledge. It is the science of life. I studied it and lived it in India for many years, and it is the ground my clinic stands on.

It sees the whole person: your unique nature, your prakriti; the balance of your three vital energies, vata, pitta and kapha; your digestion, your mind and your spirit. It does not separate the body from the emotions, or the emotions from the way you live.

For Ayurveda, health is your natural state. What we call illness begins much earlier, as a quiet imbalance that builds up in the body, in the emotions and in the way we live. That is why Ayurveda listens to the small signs, while it is still simple to return to your center.

THE FOUNDATIONS

Five elements, three doshas

For Ayurveda, everything that exists, outside you and within you, is made of five elements: space, air, fire, water and earth. In the body they come together as three vital forces, the doshas. Each one governs a part of life, and all three live in you in a proportion that is yours alone. Knowing that measure is the beginning of knowing yourself.

AIR AND SPACE

Vata

Everything that moves in you: your breathing, your circulation, the impulses of your nerves, elimination. In your mind, creativity and enthusiasm. When it is disturbed, you feel it as restlessness, dryness and fatigue.

FIRE AND WATER

Pitta

Everything that is transformed in you: your digestion, your metabolism, the heat of your body, sight. In your mind, intelligence and determination. When it is disturbed, you feel it as heat, acidity and irritation.

EARTH AND WATER

Kapha

What gives you form and holds you up: your tissues, your joints, your strength and your defenses. In your mind, calm, patience and love. When it is disturbed, you feel it as heaviness, slowness and attachment.

THREE WORDS THAT EXPLAIN ALMOST EVERYTHING

Agni, ama and ojas

There are three words that come up in every one of my consultations, because so much of the path to health is held in them.

THE FIRE

Agni

Your digestive fire. It digests not only what you eat, but also what you feel and what you live through. When agni is strong and steady, what you live becomes nourishment, clarity and strength.

WHAT WAS NOT DIGESTED

Ama

What the fire could not digest. A heavy, sticky residue that settles in the tissues and channels, and that lies at the root of almost every imbalance. Panchakarma exists to release it.

HOW AN IMBALANCE IS BORN

The six stages

Ayurveda can see an imbalance long before it becomes a problem.

Tradition describes six stages that a disturbed dosha passes through. I tell you about them because knowing them changes the way you care for yourself: in the first stages, returning to balance is simple; the further the process goes, the more time and patience it asks for.

  1. Accumulation (sanchaya). The dosha begins to build up in its own place. It is barely noticeable: a little gas or dryness if it is vata, a little heat if it is pitta, a little heaviness if it is kapha. Already at this stage the body asks you for the opposite of what disturbed it.
  2. Aggravation (prakopa). The dosha keeps growing and stirs in its place. The signs become clearer, but they still settle with changes in food and routine.
  3. Spreading (prasara). The dosha overflows its place and begins to circulate through the body, looking for somewhere to settle.
  4. Localization (sthana samshraya). It settles wherever it finds a weakened tissue or channel, often together with ama. That is where what will later become a symptom begins to form.
  5. Manifestation (vyakti). The imbalance becomes visible: now you do notice it, with its own signs.
  6. Differentiation (bheda). The imbalance takes its final form, and caring for it asks for more time and more constancy.

That is why, in the consultation, I pay so much attention to the small signs: the tongue, the pulse, sleep, digestion, mood. Ayurveda is, before anything else, a medicine that prevents.

Ayurveda works in three movements. First it comes to know your nature and returns each dosha to its place. When it is needed, it cleanses from the root what the body has been holding. And then it nourishes, so that what was cleansed fills with life.

YOUR CONSULTATION

How I come to know you

Before I recommend anything, I need to know you.

  1. I listen. We talk about your life: your digestion, your sleep, your energy through the day, your emotions, your history and what brings you here.
  2. I read your pulse. In pulse reading, nadi pariksha, three fingers on the wrist listen to vata, pitta and kapha. The pulse tells what you are, and also what is out of place today.
  3. I observe. The tongue, the eyes, the skin, the nails, the voice and the way you move say a great deal about your nature and about the presence of ama.
  4. I understand your nature. I recognize your prakriti, the constitution you were born with, and your vikriti, the state you are in today. The path is to bring you back to yourself.
  5. We map out your path. Food, routine, plants and therapies chosen for you. If it is the moment, a Panchakarma or a Rasayana. And when it helps, we draw on the clinic's evaluations.
  6. I walk with you. Balance is cared for over time. We meet again, adjust what is needed and go on.

THE THERAPIES

Hands, oils and plants

I choose every therapy for your nature and your moment. These are some of the ones I use most.

OIL MASSAGE

Abhyanga

Warm medicated oils, chosen for your dosha. They nourish the skin, calm the nervous system and soften what the body is holding. In Sanskrit, sneha means both oil and love.

THE STREAM OF OIL

Shirodhara

A continuous stream of warm oil flowing over the forehead. The mind quiets, the breath slows and a very deep calm appears. It is one of the most beautiful therapies in Ayurveda.

THE WARMTH OF HERBS

Swedana

Herbal steam that opens the body's channels. After the oil, it helps what was held to loosen and move toward where it can leave.

THE DOOR OF THE HEAD

Nasya

Medicated oils received through the nose, the door of the head. They clear the head and the senses, and support a clear mind.

THE VITAL POINTS

Marma

The points where energy, tissue and consciousness meet. Touched with attention, they let energy flow again and help the body remember its balance.
