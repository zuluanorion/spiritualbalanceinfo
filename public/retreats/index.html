THE HEALER OF CHIANG MAI

Pakkapron

Born in 1946 in the village of Namjum, in the hills of Chiang Mai, Pakkapron discovered her gift when a young girl suffering from seizures arrived at her door. Since then, for more than three decades, she has channeled the energy of the Medicine Buddha to heal, to teach the Dharma and to offer spiritual guidance, freely, to thousands of people from around the world.
